About Crystal AI

Crystal AI — Smart Live Learning & Virtual Classroom.

Crystal AI is a modern SaaS virtual education platform connecting instructors and students through seamless real-time video classrooms.

Our Mission

Online education should be effortless, secure, and engaging. We built Crystal AI to eliminate high-friction downloads, complicated meeting links, and fragmented attendance recording. With WebRTC-powered live video directly inside modern web browsers, teachers start teaching in one click and students focus on learning.

For Educators

Host live classes with audio/video and screen share, take instant roll call with automated attendance duration tracking, manage student enrollment, and download CSV reports.

For Students

Join live classrooms securely via 6-character room codes or Google one-click login, participate in real-time moderated chat, and check attendance metrics across all courses.

Core Technology Stack
  • LiveKit & WebRTC: High-definition video and low-latency audio streaming directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari.
  • Python & Django 5: Robust role-based access control, cryptographic session handling, and production-tested security hardening.
  • Automated Attendance Engine: Real-time duration tracking, presence percentage thresholds, and CSV analytics.
  • Responsive Modern UI: Clean Bootstrap 5 design optimized across smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations.
