Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Crystal AI Platform

1. Information We Collect

Crystal AI collects information necessary to deliver educational video classrooms and administrative analytics: account profile details (name, email, institutional affiliation), class enrollments, live session attendance duration, and teacher-student chat messages during live classes.

2. Authentication & Google OAuth

When students authenticate using Google OAuth, we receive authorized profile credentials (name, verified email, and avatar picture). We do not store or request access to your private Google drive or external documents.

3. Live Video & Audio Data

Video and audio streams are processed in real-time via end-to-end encrypted LiveKit WebRTC media servers. Crystal AI does not record or store student video streams unless explicitly authorized by course administrative policy.

4. Attendance & Academic Records

Attendance logs (join timestamps, departure timestamps, presence duration, and session percentages) are recorded for academic accountability and accessible strictly to the class teacher and authorized institutional administrators.

5. Data Protection & Security

We enforce HTTPS/HSTS, secure session cookies, strict Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and CSRF mitigation, and role-based access control across all API endpoints and views.

6. Contact for Inquiries

For data access or privacy inquiries, contact our data protection officer at privacy@teachlive.edu.
