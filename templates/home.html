Explore the core modules built specifically for high-engagement online classrooms.

Classroom Hub & Code Join

Teachers generate unique 6-character classroom codes. Students enroll in 1 click without complicated invitation chains or administrative overhead.

Attendance Logging

Take roll call per lecture session with Present, Absent, Late, or Excused status. Automated percentage calculation and instant student attendance records.

Classroom Discussion

Engage in real-time academic discussion and Q&A streams within each classroom. Teacher and student messages are distinctly identified.

Schedules & Timetable

Publish recurring lecture times, lab sessions, and office hours with clear weekly timetables so students never miss an important meeting.

Role-Based Security

Strict role separation for ADMIN, TEACHER, and STUDENT users. Views and endpoints enforce role verification with custom permissions.

REST API Architecture

Built-in Django REST Framework endpoints for users, classrooms, attendance records, and chat messages for seamless frontend-backend decoupling.
